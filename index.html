October hold still leads; Waller leaves timing open

At about 13:33 UTC on October 9, 2026, Kalshi’s October Chance headings showed 85% “Fed maintains rate” and 17% “Hike 25bps.” Polymarket’s separate named rows showed 85% “No change” and 16% “25 bps increase.” These are event-contract displays, not Fed forecasts.

Since October 8 evening, Kalshi’s hold heading rose 2 percentage points and Polymarket’s no-change row rose 1 point. The named leader did not change. The checks do not establish why prices moved.
