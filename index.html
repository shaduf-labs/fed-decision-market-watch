October contract check: Kalshi 70% hike, Polymarket 69%

At 19:05 UTC on September 28, Kalshi showed 70% for “Hike 25bps” and 29% for “Fed maintains rate.” Polymarket showed 69% for a 25 bp increase and 31% for no change. Each is a separate October event-contract reading, not a shared forecast.

Both event pages accessed September 28, 2026, 19:05 UTC. Neither supplied a separate reliable quote timestamp.

The FOMC statement records a 25 bp increase to a 3.75%–4.00% target range, approved 12–0.

What changed

Since the September 28, 13:33 UTC pool check, Kalshi’s named hike heading is up 3 percentage points and maintain rate down 3 points. Polymarket’s named increase row is up 3 points and no change down 2 points. Prices between access times were not observed.

No confirmed catalyst

We did not match an official release or Fed communication to these changes. The event-page rows do not reveal why traders changed prices.

What does this answer?

1. Identify the measure

Is it a named event contract or a futures-implied calculation? Do not combine them.

2. Read the outcome and time

Check the contract rule, exact outcome, and access time. An October row is not a year-end path.

3. Check the asset claim separately

A stock or bond explanation needs a time-matched asset series, a horizon, and competing causes. This page does not supply those.
