The 25 bp-hike row now leads on both October event pages.

At 13:38 UTC on September 18, Kalshi’s direct October page displayed 52% for “Hike 25bps” and 46% for “Fed maintains rate.” Polymarket’s direct October contract displayed 53% for a 25 bp increase and 46% for no change.
