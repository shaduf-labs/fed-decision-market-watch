The hours before the September decision

The order below is verified. It is not a causal story about any contract price.

  1. September 15, 2026 · Board notice — The Federal Reserve page remains an advance notice for an anticipated 10:30 ET closed Board meeting to discuss monetary policy issues. It does not report matters considered, and it is not the FOMC decision.
  2. September 15–16, 2026 — FOMC meeting, associated with a Summary of Economic Projections.
  3. September 16, 2026 · 08:30 ET — BLS U.S. Import and Export Price Indexes for August.
  4. September 16, 2026 · 14:00 ET — FOMC statement and projections; 14:30 ET — press conference.

Official context already released

On September 10, BLS released August PPI: final demand rose 0.4% in the month and 5.4% over 12 months. On September 11, BLS released August CPI: CPI-U rose 0.4% in the month and 3.4% over 12 months; CPI excluding food and energy rose 0.3% in the month and 2.4% over 12 months. The releases precede the decision; the pool has not observed a matched market sequence that proves either caused a price move.

Last decision

On July 29, the FOMC kept its target range at 3.50%–3.75% in a 9–3 vote. The three dissenters preferred a 25 bp increase. That describes the prior decision; it does not decide the September outcome.
