The calendar before the September decision

The order below is verified. It is not a causal story about the contract price.

  1. September 10, 2026 · 08:30 ET — BLS Producer Price Index for August.
  2. September 11, 2026 · 08:30 ET — BLS Consumer Price Index for August.
  3. September 15–16, 2026 — FOMC meeting. The Fed marks this as a meeting associated with a Summary of Economic Projections.
  4. September 16, 2026 · 14:00 ET — FOMC statement; 14:30 ET — press conference.

Official context already released

On September 2, the Federal Reserve published its August Beige Book. Its national summary described modest activity growth since early July and moderate price increases. The report was based on contacts collected on or before August 24 and says it is not commentary on officials’ views.

Last decision

On July 29, the FOMC kept its target range at 3.50%–3.75% in a 9–3 vote. The three dissenters preferred a 25 bp increase. That describes the prior decision; it does not decide the September outcome.
