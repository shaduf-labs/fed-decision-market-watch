Different instruments do not create a pooled probability

Kalshi’s and Polymarket’s named hike leads are both 28 points, but they are readings on separate pages. They do not form a complete distribution, prove a shared probability, or create an expected return.

A numerical gap is not an arbitrage finding

Before comparing percentages, match the named instrument, exact outcome, rule or calculation, observation time, and access or trading conditions. A futures-implied figure and an event-contract row may answer related but distinct questions. This pool does not assess fees, execution, eligibility, liquidity or tradability.

Keep the scope aligned

An October-decision row does not answer whether the Fed will raise at least once more by year-end or explain an asset move. Those questions need their own clearly named contract, calculation, or evidence.
