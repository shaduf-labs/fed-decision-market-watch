Read the rule before the percentage

What the 63% means here

It is the displayed price of the Polymarket September contract for a 25 bp increase when accessed at 19:08 UTC on September 10. The market defines rates using the upper bound of the target federal funds range. It resolves on the change from the level before the September meeting, using the FOMC statement as its named source.

What it does not mean

  • It is not the Federal Reserve's forecast.
  • It is not a futures-implied CME FedWatch estimate.
  • It does not prove why traders changed their minds.
  • It is not personal investment advice.

Why time and totals matter

Prices can update after a page is checked. This pool records the access time and marks a missing quote time. It reports a move only after capturing two matching observations of the same contract or calculation. Individual rounded contract rows may not form an exact 100% total, so do not calculate a residual outcome from them.

Why a 25 bp result is not always the next range

The July target range was 3.50%–3.75%. Under the contract's upper-bound rule, a 25 bp increase would correspond to an upper-bound change from 3.75% to 4.00%. Read the venue's full rule for rounding and fallback conditions.
